**1. General Provisions**

**1.1.** This document describes two processes: how to report a
rule-breaker (a report) and how to contest a penalty (an appeal).

**1.2.** Reports and appeals are working tools, not a way to pressure
the team. Abusing them is punished.

**1.3.** All decisions on reports and appeals rest on evidence and the
game rules.

**1.4.** This document applies alongside the in-game rules and the team
charter.

**2. Where and What to Submit**

> *Reports on rule-breakers are submitted in the game. Appeals and
> questions go to Discord. Don't mix the channels: a report in Discord
> and an appeal in the game will not be reviewed.*

**2.1. Reports — in the game**

**2.1.1.** Reports on cheaters and any other violations are submitted
directly in the game.

**2.1.2.** A report is for when a player breaks the rules: cheats,
exploits, bug abuse, scamming, toxicity, and so on.

**2.2. Appeals and questions — in Discord**

**2.2.1.** Only appeals (contesting a ban) and support questions are
submitted in Discord.

**2.2.2.** Support answers questions about the game and the server under
a separate support regulation.

**3. How to File a Report**

*The fuller the report, the faster and more accurate the decision.
Incomplete reports may be rejected.*

**3.1. What Must Be Included**

**3.1.1.** The rule-breaker's nickname (their display name in the game).

**3.1.2.** The rule-breaker's username (@username on Roblox).

**3.1.3.** The rule-breaker's current ID.

**3.1.4.** A clip with evidence (YouTube / Medal / Discord).

**3.1.5.** A description of the situation — what exactly happened and
which rule was broken.

**3.1.6.** The approximate time it happened. If the time is stated in
the clip's title — even better.

**3.2. Evidence Requirements**

**3.2.1.** Evidence means a video clip, not just words or a screenshot.

**3.2.2.** The clip must clearly show the violation; explain context in
the description if needed.

**3.2.3.** A report without a clip as evidence will not be reviewed.

**4. What Happens to a Report**

**4.1.** Moderation reviews the report and the evidence.

**4.2.** If the violation is confirmed, the player is penalized under
the game rules.

**4.3.** If the evidence is insufficient, the report may be rejected
with a note on the reason.

**4.4.** The report's author may not demand a specific penalty —
moderation decides it.

**4.5.** The details of the internal review are not disclosed.

**5. False Reports and Spam**

> *Reports exist to catch rule-breakers, not to bury moderation.
> Knowingly false reports and spam are punished.*

**5.1. Forbidden**

**5.1.1.** Filing knowingly false reports against innocent players.

**5.1.2.** Spamming reports and deliberately overloading moderation.

**5.1.3.** Faking or editing evidence.

**5.1.4.** Using reports for revenge or to pressure other players.

**5.2. Penalty**

**5.2.1.** For a first false report or spam — a warning.

**5.2.2.** For repeats — a mute in the report channels, then a block on
the ability to file reports.

**5.2.3.** Faking evidence is treated as a serious violation and
punished more harshly.

**6. Appeals**

*An appeal is a way to contest a ban, not to argue with moderation.
Submit it calmly and to the point.*

> *Appeals are submitted only to administrators and only about unbans.
> Appeals on other matters (mutes, kicks, warnings) are not reviewed
> through this channel.*

**6.1. To Whom and About What**

**6.1.1.** An appeal is submitted only to administrators.

**6.1.2.** An appeal is only about an unban (lifting a ban).

**6.1.3.** An appeal makes sense if the player believes they were banned
falsely, or believes they have reformed.

**6.2. How to Submit**

**6.2.1.** State your nickname and username.

**6.2.2.** State what the ban was for and roughly when it was issued.

**6.2.3.** Calmly explain why the ban should be lifted; attaching your
own evidence helps.

**6.2.4.** Insults and pressure in an appeal forfeit its review.

**6.3. How Many Attempts**

**6.3.1.** For ordinary (temporary) bans, an appeal may be re-submitted
if new arguments appear or the player believes they have reformed.

**6.3.2.** For a permanent ban, only one appeal attempt is given.

**6.3.3.** Repeat appeals on a permanent ban after a refusal are not
reviewed.

**6.4. Outcome**

**6.4.1.** If a false ban is confirmed, the penalty is lifted and
removed property is returned where possible.

**6.4.2.** If the ban is found fair, the appeal is rejected with an
explanation.

**6.4.3.** An administrator's decision on an appeal is final within this
process.

**7. Timeframes**

*Timeframes are soft and serve as a guideline, not a strict obligation.*

**7.1.** Reports are usually reviewed within a few days; complex cases
take longer.

**7.2.** Appeals are usually reviewed within about a week.

**7.3.** No reply within the rough timeframe does not mean an automatic
decision in anyone's favor — wait calmly.

**7.4.** Repeated reminders and spam do not speed up the review.

**8. Final Provisions**

**8.1.** Reports and appeals work only when used honestly.

**8.2.** Moderation and administration may act on the spirit of the
rules in situations not directly described.

**8.3.** This document may change; changes take effect from the moment
of publication.

**8.4.** Not knowing this document does not exempt you from
responsibility.

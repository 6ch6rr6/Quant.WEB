**1. General Provisions**

**1.1.** These rules govern player conduct inside the game servers and
apply to everyone without exception.

**1.2.** By starting to play, you automatically agree to these rules. “I
didn't know the rules” is not an excuse.

**1.3.** These rules apply alongside the Roblox platform rules
(Community Standards and Terms of Use). What is forbidden on Roblox is
forbidden here.

**1.4.** The rules cannot describe every possible situation. Moderation
may act on the spirit of the rules, not only the letter.

**1.5.** Not knowing the updated rules does not exempt you from
responsibility.

**2. Fair Play (Cheats and Exploits)**

*The most serious block. Any unfair advantage over other players is
forbidden.*

> *Cheats, exploits, and script injectors = instant permanent ban. An
> appeal is possible only if the player believes they were banned
> falsely (see section 8).*

**2.1. What Is Forbidden**

**2.1.1.** Using any cheats, scripts, injectors, or third-party software
that grants an advantage.

**2.1.2.** Exploiting game vulnerabilities to gain an advantage over
other players.

**2.1.3.** Aimbot, wallhack, speedhack, teleports, flight,
invulnerability, and any similar modifications.

**2.1.4.** Attempting to harm allies through workarounds (exploits,
mechanic abuse), since killing your own side is not possible in-game.

**2.1.5.** Distributing, advertising, or explaining cheats and exploits
to other players.

**2.2. Penalty**

**2.2.1.** For cheats and exploits — a permanent ban without warning.

**2.2.2.** An appeal is possible only if the player is confident the ban
was false; they may request a re-review of the case (section 8).

**3. Use of Bugs**

*Bugs are game errors, not cheats. But profiting from them silently is
not allowed.*

> *Found a bug — report it. Stayed silent and profited from it (in PvP,
> earnings, progress, anything) — that's a ban with the option to
> appeal.*

**3.1. The Bug Rule**

**3.1.1.** On finding a bug, a player must report it to the team (to bug
reports), not use it.

**3.1.2.** Bug reports are welcome and encouraged.

**3.1.3.** Deliberately using a bug for any advantage — in PvP,
earnings, progress, or otherwise — is forbidden.

**3.1.4.** Hiding a bug and profiting from it counts as a violation.

**3.2. Penalty**

**3.2.1.** For using a bug for advantage and hiding it — a ban with the
option to appeal.

**3.2.2.** An honest player who reports a bug is not penalized, even if
they stumbled onto it by accident.

**4. Conduct and Communication**

**4.1. General Requirements**

**4.1.1.** Respect other players. Toxicity, harassment, and provocations
ruin the game for everyone.

**4.1.2.** Insults, threats, and incitement of hostility on any grounds
are forbidden.

**4.1.3.** Spam, flooding, and abuse of voice or text chat in-game are
forbidden.

**4.1.4.** 18+ content, shock content, and anything that breaks Roblox
rules are forbidden.

**4.2. In-Game Behavior**

**4.2.1.** Deliberate sabotage of your own faction and allies through
workarounds is forbidden.

**4.2.2.** Deliberately disrupting NPC mechanics and events to ruin the
game for others is forbidden.

**4.2.3.** Impersonating a team member, developer, or moderator is
forbidden.

**4.2.4.** Begging staff for items, currency, or advantages is
forbidden.

**5. Economy, Trading, and Progress**

*Everything tied to earnings, items, and advancement in the game.*

**5.1. Forbidden**

**5.1.1.** Scamming: deceiving other players in exchanges, deals, or
agreements inside the game.

**5.1.2.** Selling, trading, or transferring in-game items, currency, or
accounts for real money or Robux outside official systems.

**5.1.3.** Abusing game mechanics and events for abnormal farming of
resources, currency, or progress.

**5.1.4.** Using economy errors (duping items or currency) — this is
also a violation of section 3.

**5.2. Penalty**

**5.2.1.** For scamming — a penalty along the ladder (section 7); for
large or repeated scams, up to a permanent ban.

**5.2.2.** For duping and economy abuse — a ban with the option to
appeal; unfairly gained property is removed or reset.

**6. Accounts and Ban Evasion**

> *A penalty is given to the person, not just the account. Ban evasion
> and alt accounts are punished harshly.*

**6.1. Rules**

**6.1.1.** Evading a ban through other accounts (ban evasion) is
forbidden.

**6.1.2.** Using alt accounts to gain advantages, manipulate, or bypass
the rules is forbidden.

**6.1.3.** A player is responsible for everything that happens on their
account; “it was my brother/friend” does not remove responsibility.

**6.1.4.** Handing over an account to evade a penalty is forbidden.

**6.2. Penalty**

**6.2.1.** Ban evasion extends or hardens the original penalty and leads
to a ban of all linked accounts.

**6.2.2.** Alt accounts for advantages — a ban of all involved accounts.

**7. Penalty Ladder**

*A guideline for moderation. Durations are reasonable, not dragged out;
severity depends on the violation.*

**7.1. Standard Ladder (for behavioral violations)**

**7.1.1.** Warning — for a first or minor violation.

**7.1.2.** In-game chat mute for 1–24 hours — for spam, toxicity, and
repeated minor violations.

**7.1.3.** Kick from the server — for continuing a violation after a
warning.

**7.1.4.** Temporary ban for 1–7 days — for serious or repeated
violations.

**7.1.5.** Temporary ban for 7–30 days — for gross violations or
systematic behavior.

**7.1.6.** Permanent ban — for the most serious violations or persistent
disregard of the rules.

**7.2. Instant Permanent Ban (no ladder)**

**7.2.1.** Cheats, exploits, script injectors.

**7.2.2.** Large-scale scamming or systematic deception of players.

**7.2.3.** Threats, harvesting personal data, 18+ content.

**7.2.4.** Persistent ban evasion.

**7.3. Ban With the Option to Appeal**

**7.3.1.** Using a bug for advantage and hiding it (section 3).

**7.3.2.** Duping and economy abuse (section 5).

**7.3.3.** Disputed cases where the player believes the penalty is
mistaken.

**7.4. General Principles**

**7.4.1.** The severity of the penalty matches the severity of the
violation; moderation considers the player's history.

**7.4.2.** Repeated violations harden the penalty.

**7.4.3.** The stated durations are a guideline, not a rigid dogma;
extreme cases are decided by the spirit of the rules.

**8. Evidence and Appeals**

**8.1. Evidence**

**8.1.1.** A penalty is given only with evidence: a clip on YouTube /
Medal / Discord, with a description of the situation if needed.

**8.1.2.** Penalizing “by eye,” without evidence, is not allowed and may
be overturned.

**8.2. Appeals**

**8.2.1.** If a player believes they were banned falsely, they may file
an appeal and request a re-review of the case.

**8.2.2.** An appeal is submitted calmly, stating the username and the
circumstances; attaching your own evidence helps.

**8.2.3.** If a false ban is confirmed, the penalty is lifted and
removed property is returned where possible.

**8.2.4.** Insults and pressure in an appeal forfeit its review.

**9. Final Provisions**

**9.1.** Fair play and respect for others are the foundation of the
community.

**9.2.** Moderation may act on the spirit of the rules in situations not
directly described.

**9.3.** The rules may change; changes take effect from the moment of
publication.

**9.4.** Not knowing the rules does not exempt you from responsibility.

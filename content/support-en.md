**1. General Provisions**

**1.1.** This document describes what you can and cannot bring to
support.

**1.2.** Support helps players with game questions and technical
problems, but does not handle moderation or penalties.

**1.3.** Support operates alongside the in-game rules, the
reports-and-appeals document, and the team charter.

**1.4.** Not knowing this document does not exempt you from
responsibility for your conduct in the support channels.

**2. What You Can Bring**

*Support is about help with the game and technical matters. Anything not
about staff and not in the wiki.*

> *The rule is simple: you may ask anything about the game that isn't in
> the wiki, and anything technical — as long as the question isn't about
> staff.*

**2.1. Examples of Allowed Requests**

**2.1.1.** Any questions about the game that the wiki does not answer.

**2.1.2.** Game problems: crashes, freezes, errors, technical faults.

**2.1.3.** Items not being granted due to a bug, and similar failures.

**2.1.4.** Mentioning bugs and reporting technical problems.

**2.1.5.** Other technical questions related to the game.

**2.2. General Principle**

**2.2.1.** If a question is about the game and is not in the wiki,
support will try to help.

**2.2.2.** If a problem is technical or caused by a bug, support will
take the request and pass it to development if needed.

**3. First — the Game Wiki**

> *Before writing to support, check the game wiki. If the answer is
> there, support will simply redirect you to it.*

**3.1.** The game wiki is the main source of answers on mechanics,
items, and how the game works.

**3.2.** Questions already answered in the wiki are not handled in
detail by support — you'll be pointed to the wiki.

**3.3.** This saves time for both you and the team: support focuses on
what the wiki doesn't cover.

**4. What NOT to Bring**

*Some questions aren't for support. Support will redirect you to the
right place.*

**4.1. Not for Support**

**4.1.1.** Questions answered in the game wiki — check the wiki first.

**4.1.2.** Any questions about staff: team roster, staff penalties,
internal affairs — not for support.

**4.1.3.** Contesting a ban is an appeal; it goes to administrators
under the reports-and-appeals document.

**4.1.4.** A complaint about a rule-breaking player is a report; it is
filed in the game under the reports document.

**4.2. What Support Does With Such Requests**

**4.2.1.** Support will politely redirect you to the right channel or
role.

**4.2.2.** Support does not issue penalties, lift bans, or take reports
in place of the dedicated channels.

**5. “When Will X Be Added / Fixed” Questions**

> *Questions like “when will X be added” and “when will Y be fixed” are
> refused outright. This is not for support.*

**5.1. The Rule**

**5.1.1.** Questions about when something will be added to the game are
not handled by support and are refused.

**5.1.2.** Questions about when something will be fixed are also
refused.

**5.1.3.** Support does not give dates or promises about future content
and fixes.

**5.2. Where to Take These**

**5.2.1.** Suggestions for new content go to the idea forum under its
rules.

**5.2.2.** Requests for fixes are also filed as an idea on the forum,
not as a support question.

**5.2.3.** The bug itself can and should be mentioned to support — just
without the “when will you fix it” question.

**6. How to Reach Out Properly**

*The clearer the request, the faster the help.*

**6.1.** Describe the problem or question clearly and to the point.

**6.2.** For technical problems and bugs, add details: what happened,
when, and what you were doing at the time.

**6.3.** Attach a screenshot or clip if you can — it speeds up the
review.

**6.4.** One question, one request; don't pile everything together.

**6.5.** Wait calmly for a reply; repeated reminders and spam do not
speed up help.

**7. Conduct in Support**

**7.1.** Be polite with support; support staff are people too.

**7.2.** Insults, pressure, and demands toward support are forbidden.

**7.3.** Spamming requests and deliberately overloading support are
forbidden.

**7.4.** Begging support for items, currency, or advantages is
forbidden.

**7.5.** Misconduct in the support channels is punished under the
general rules.

**8. Final Provisions**

**8.1.** Support exists to help players, not to replace the dedicated
channels.

**8.2.** Support may redirect requests and act on the spirit of the
rules in situations not described.

**8.3.** This document may change; changes take effect from the moment
of publication.

**8.4.** Not knowing this document does not exempt you from
responsibility.
